# AI Readiness Scorecard

A free, interactive AI readiness scorecard for business owners. Answer 12 short questions and see how ready your business is for AI.

- One score out of 100 and a short result
- No email needed, and nothing is saved or sent. Everything runs in the browser
- A single file: open `index.html` to use it
